💡 The AI Ambassador Concept

🌍 What are AI Ambassadors?

AI Ambassadors are branded AI experiences that transcend cultural and language barriers. Instead of technical "agents," they're friendly representatives that can be visualized as anything: emojis, creatures, shapes, wearable designs, or even bugs!

🎮 The Pokemon GO Connection

Just like Pokemon GO brought virtual creatures into the physical world, AI Ambassadors bring AI experiences into physical spaces. Scan a code, enter a world, and interact with an AI personality - all seamlessly blending digital and physical.

🏪 Retail Store:
QR code on product → Meet "ShopBot" ambassador → Get styling advice & recommendations
🎨 Art Gallery:
QR code next to artwork → Meet "ArtGuide" ambassador → Learn history & techniques
